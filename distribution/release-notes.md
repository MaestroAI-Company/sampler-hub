## Models you can trust

The llama.cpp model list is back to a short list of starter models picked by hand, flagged for the memory of your machine. It no longer loads from Hugging Face.

## Share models on their own

Turning off **Share providers** no longer hides the models your hub runs with llama.cpp. It only covers Ollama and LM Studio on the hub machine, the models setting decides for llama.cpp.
